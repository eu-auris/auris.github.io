Eligibility

To be eligible for an MSCA Doctoral Network position, applicants must, on the date of recruitment ({% include v.html value=r.recruitment_date %}):

  • {% include icon.html name="check" %}Not already hold a doctoral degree.
  • {% include icon.html name="check" %}Comply with the MSCA mobility rule: not have resided or carried out their main activity (work, studies, etc.) in the country of the recruiting organisation for more than 12 months in the 36 months immediately before the recruitment date.
  • {% include icon.html name="check" %}Be eligible to enrol in a doctoral programme at the PhD-awarding institution of the position.
  • {% include icon.html name="check" %}Applicants of any nationality may apply.
